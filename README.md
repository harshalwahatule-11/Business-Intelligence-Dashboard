📊 Business Intelligence Dashboard (Excel)

📌 Project Overview

The Business Intelligence Dashboard is an interactive, Excel-based Business Intelligence solution developed to monitor, analyze, and visualize business performance across sales, revenue, profit, customer segments, products, and regions.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and interactive Slicers. It helps users identify business trends, evaluate performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall business performance.

Monitor net revenue, total profit, and average profit margin.

Identify top-performing products based on revenue.

Compare sales and profit performance across regions.

Analyze revenue contribution from different customer segments.

Monitor monthly revenue trends.

Evaluate sales representative performance.

Support strategic business decisions through interactive visualizations.

💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following insights were identified:

Top Revenue Category: Apparel contributes the highest revenue.

Regional Performance: The West region generates the highest profit.

Customer Segment: Online customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.

Revenue Trends: Monthly revenue patterns reveal potential seasonal business trends.

Sales Performance: Top-performing sales representatives contribute a significant share of revenue.

Category Profitability: Profit margins vary across product categories, providing opportunities for further profitability analysis.

These insights can help businesses evaluate performance, understand revenue contributions, and identify areas for improvement.

🚀 How to Use the Dashboard

Download or clone this repository to your local computer.

Locate the Excel dashboard file in the repository.

Open the workbook using Microsoft Excel 2016 or a later compatible version.

Use the Product Category and Product Name Slicers to filter the dashboard.

Review the KPIs, charts, and revenue trends.

Compare product, regional, customer segment, and sales representative performance.

Use the insights to support business analysis and decision-making.

Note: The interactive functionality depends on the workbook containing the configured Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.
