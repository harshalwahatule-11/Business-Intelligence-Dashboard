📊 Business Intelligence Dashboard (Excel)

📌 Project Overview

An interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze business performance across sales, revenue, profit, products, regions, and customer segments. It transforms raw data into actionable insights using KPIs, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.

🎯 Key Features

KPIs: Net Revenue, Total Profit, and Average Profit Margin

Product Analysis: Sales by category and top 10 products by revenue

Regional Analysis: Revenue and profit performance across regions

Trend Analysis: Monthly revenue trends

Customer Insights: Revenue distribution by customer segment

Sales Performance: Top sales representatives by revenue

Interactive Filters: Filter data by product category and product name

💡 Key Insights

Apparel contributes the highest revenue.

The West region generates the highest profit.

Online customers contribute significantly to revenue.

Monthly revenue trends reveal seasonal patterns.

Profit margins vary across product categories.

🚀 How to Use

Download the Excel dashboard from this repository.

Open it in Microsoft Excel 2016 or later.

Use the slicers to filter by product category and product name.

Explore the KPIs and interactive charts to understand business performance.
